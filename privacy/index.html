Privacy Policy

Cozy Pomo for iPhone. Effective 12 October 2026.

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-up.
  • Your timer settings, sessions, statistics and session tags stay on your iPhone.
  • The app sends anonymous usage statistics so we can see which features are used. They contain no identifier of you or your device.
  • There are no ads, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising or tracking.

Information that stays on your iPhone

The app stores the following on your device only. It is never sent to us:

  • Your settings, such as durations, theme and colour.
  • The state of your current timer.
  • Today's focus statistics. These are cleared automatically at midnight.
  • The text of any tag you give a session.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends short records of how it is used. We use them only to understand which features are used and to decide what to improve.

What is sentExamples
Actions in the appThe app was opened; a plan was started, finished or reset; a break was skipped; a setting or theme was changed; a screen was opened; a gesture was used.
Numbers describing those actionsSession length in minutes, number of sessions, the hour of the day (0 to 23) a plan started.
About a tagOnly whether a tag was added and whether it was short, medium or long. Never the text.
About the app and deviceApp version, iOS major version, whether the device is a phone, and the country from your iPhone's region setting.
TimeWhen the action happened.

What is not collected

  • No name, email address or other contact details.
  • No advertising identifier, device identifier or install identifier. Two records from the same iPhone cannot be told apart from records from two different iPhones.
  • No IP address is read or stored by our service.
  • No location. The country comes from your region setting, not from GPS or your network.
  • No session tag text.
  • No contacts, photos, health data or anything else on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules them on your iPhone to tell you when a session ends. They are created and delivered on the device. No notification data is sent to us.

Where the statistics are kept, and for how long

The anonymous statistics are stored with Amazon Web Services in the United Kingdom (London region), which acts as our hosting provider. Records are deleted automatically 400 days after they are created. Technical server logs are kept for 14 days and do not include IP addresses.

Sharing

We do not sell the statistics and do not share them with advertisers, data brokers or analytics companies. The app contains no third-party advertising or tracking software.

Your choices and rights

Because the statistics carry no identifier, we cannot tell which records came from you. That also means we cannot look up, export or delete records for a particular person: there is nothing linking them to anyone.

Everything that is linked to you is on your iPhone and under your control. Delete the app to remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.
