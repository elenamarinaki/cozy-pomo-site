Support

Help with Cozy Pomo for iPhone.

Get in touch

Found a bug, or have a question or a suggestion? Open an issue and describe what happened. Including your iPhone model and iOS version helps. You need a free GitHub account to post.

Please do not include personal information in an issue: issues are public.

Common questions

The buttons have disappeared. How do I get them back?

The buttons are hidden when "Show timer buttons" is turned off. While they are hidden, the screen takes taps instead:

  • Tap once to start or pause.
  • Tap twice to reset. The app asks you to confirm first.
  • Tap three times to open Settings.

To bring the buttons back, tap three times, open the Controls tab, and turn on "Show timer buttons".

How do I change the length of a session?

Open Settings and use the Timer tab. In single-session mode you can also drag up or down on the timer screen before the session starts.

Durations cannot be changed once a plan has started. Reset the timer first.

I do not hear a sound when a session ends.

  • In the app: Settings, Controls tab, turn on "Sound & notifications".
  • In iPhone Settings: Notifications, Cozy Pomo, allow notifications and sounds. The app needs this to alert you while it is closed or the phone is locked.
  • Check that your iPhone is not in silent mode or a Focus that silences notifications.

Where did yesterday's statistics go?

Statistics cover today only and reset on their own at midnight. Nothing is kept beyond the current day.

How do I delete my data?

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it. There is no account to close. See the Privacy Policy for details.
